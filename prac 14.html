information about eraser

An eraser is a stationery item used to remove marks from paper or skin. Most erasers are designed to remove pencil marks. Erasers have a rubbery consistency and come in a variety of shapes, sizes, and colors. The modern eraser is usually a mixture of an abrasive such as fine pumice, a rubbery matrix such as synthetic rubber or vinyl, and other ingredients. Some special pens contain erasable ink that can be removed by erasers >
